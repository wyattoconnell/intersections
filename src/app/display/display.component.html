How To Play

Your objective is to submit 3 words belonging to each category in as few guesses as possible.

You do so by entering guesses into the textbox at the bottom of your screen and trying to identify intersections, or words that fall into multiple categories. The more intersections you find, the less guesses you'll need to complete the puzzle.

The par value represents the lowest possible amount of guesses required to complete the puzzle. The closer you get to par, the better your score! The par value is hidden to start off, but if you get stuck, you can use the Reveal Par button or the Eye icon to see if you're on the right track.
